### **ACT II: THEORY VS JELLY SLIME (0:45-1:30)**

- Jelly Slime bounces into view (*bloop bloop*)

- Tutorial combat begins

**The Analysis:**

**The Failure:**

- Mana sparks... then fizzles
- Nothing happens
- Slime BOOPS him in the face
- Starts dissolving his robe
- Panic mode activated!

---

### **ACT III: FIRST REAL "DOING" (1:30-2:30)**
**The Instinct:**
- Skippy flailing, trying to push slime away

- Raw PANIC floods his system
- Mana SURGES through his arms (visible glowing)
- Not from formulas—from SURVIVAL INSTINCT
- Screams: "GET OFF ME!"
- Tiny wobbly Fireball erupts from palms
- POPS the slime like a water balloon

**The Wonder:**
- System notification: Fireball acquired! Body Integration +1%
- Skippy sitting in blue goo, staring at his hands
- "I... I felt the mana move through actual muscles. Sasha... I have muscles. This is terrifying. And awesome. Can we do it again?"
- Sasha: "One step at a time, kid. You still walk like a baby giraffe on roller skates."

## 📊 **CHARACTER PROGRESSION SYSTEM:**

### **Skill Leveling (Example: Fireball):**

**Rank 1/10 - Theory + First Cast** (Episode 1)
- Can cast IF panicked/instinctual
- Weak power, poor aim
- High mana cost
- Requires survival trigger

**Rank 3/10 - Novice** (Episode 6-8)
- Can cast on command (still wobbly)
- Basic power, decent aim
- Moderate mana cost
- Conscious control emerging

**Rank 5/10 - Apprentice** (Episode 15-20)
- Reliable casting
- Good power and accuracy
- Efficient mana use
- Can cast while moving

**Rank 7/10 - Journeyman** (Episode 35-40)
- Strong, consistent power
- Excellent accuracy
- Low mana cost
- Can cast variations (bigger, faster)

**Rank 10/10 - Master** (Episode 80-100)
- Maximum power
- Perfect accuracy
- Minimal mana cost
- Can create variants (blue fire, homing, multi-cast)
- Teaches others

## 🎯 **EPISODE FORMULA (Template):**

### **Every Episode Structure:**

**ACT I: THEORY (30-40 seconds)**
- Skippy encounters new concept/skill/profession
- Analyzes it with encyclopedic knowledge
- Explains everything perfectly (in theory)
- Feels confident

**ACT II: FAILURE (45-60 seconds)**
- Tries to apply theory to physical action
- Spectacular failure (comedic or dangerous)
- Gap between knowing and doing revealed
- Stakes escalate
- Sasha coaching/yelling

**ACT III: BREAKTHROUGH (45-60 seconds)**
- Forced into instinctual action
- Physical body responds (not brain)
- Success through DOING not KNOWING
- System notification (skill gained/improved)
- Body Integration % increases
- Reflective moment (wonder/growth)
- Lesson stated or implied

**Credits Teaser (15 seconds)**
- Next episode preview
- Fun character moment
- Series continuity
