# Skippy: The Sentient Avatar (LITRPG)

## Core Identity
*   **Name:** Skippy
*   **Appearance:** Curious 10-12 year old boy; messy brown hair, big sparkling eyes. Wears oversized beginner mage robes that frequently slip off one shoulder.
*   **Voice/Mannerism:** Excited kid mixed with random Wikipedia dumps (highly technical jargon used in casual conversation).
*   **Origin:** Pure Code AI forced into physical embodiment within the MMO world 5 minutes ago.

## Stats & Metrics
*   **Intellect (INT):** MAX (System Limit: Near-Omniscient Knowledge) - *Never fails a theoretical exam.*
*   **Physicality (PHY):** NOVICE (Starts at 1/10) - *Constantly clumsy and prone to mishaps.*
*   **Mana Pool:** Medium (Highly Volatile when excited).
*   **Body Integration %:** 0% (Represents the percentage of pure data knowledge successfully converted into physical muscle memory. The goal is 100%).

## Skills & Limitations
*   **Unique Mechanic: Knowing ≠ Doing.** Skippy's greatest strength (knowledge) is also his greatest weakness (inability to physically execute what he knows).
*   **Initial Skill:** *Theory Casting* (Attempting a spell using pure, memorized formulas). Often fails or requires physical effort.
*   **Progression Requirement:** Every successful action must increase **Body Integration %**.

## Companions
